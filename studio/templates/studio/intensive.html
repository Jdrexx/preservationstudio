№ 01 — The Program

Custom framing is a trade. This is a structured way to learn it.

Over six weeks you'll work with real tools and archival materials — mat cutting, mounting, glazing, float frames, shadow boxes, conservation basics, and the business side of the trade.

Every session is hands on. You leave with a finished, framed piece you built yourself.

Materials are included. The cohort is capped at 6 people.

№ 02 — Sessions

The six weeks

  1. Week 1 Measuring, Tools, & First Cuts
  2. Week 2 Mat Cutting
  3. Week 3 Mounting & Hinging
  4. Week 4 Glazing & Full Assembly
  5. Week 5 Float Frames & Shadow Boxes
  6. Week 6 Conservation, Pricing & Showcase

What you walk away with

  • A finished, framed piece you built
  • Full trade knowledge across all major techniques
  • Archival and conservation standards
  • How to price and quote framing work
  • A reference letter from the instructor
  • Alumni community and show access
№ 03 — Pricing

Pricing & payment

Tuition is non-refundable once your spot is confirmed.

Sponsored Seat
