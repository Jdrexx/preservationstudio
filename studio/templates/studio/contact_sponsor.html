Sponsor a Seat

One sponsored seat is available per cohort, reserved for a QTPOC artist demonstrating financial need and a serious commitment to the work. Full tuition covered.

№ 01 — What sponsors receive

Sponsoring organizations and donors receive named recognition on the program page and all relevant cohort materials, social media acknowledgment, and named recognition in the annual group show program.

Sponsors are invited to the end of year group show.

For grant-funded sponsorships a formal letter of acknowledgment and outcome documentation is provided for reporting purposes.

Sponsored seats are made possible through private donors, organizations, and grants. Availability varies by cohort.
